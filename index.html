      Parking Coach UK

      Learn the DVSA test manoeuvres in a right-hand-drive car: what to look for, when to turn, when to stop.

      1. Your car

      2. Gearbox

      3. Manoeuvre & mode

      Demo: the car drives itself and the camera looks where you should look. Guided: you drive, with step-by-step prompts, reference points and a ghost car. Test: no prompts; you are scored like on the test, then shown when you turned compared with the ideal.

      Easy and Automatic

      Easy: press ↑ to go forwards or ↓ to reverse; the gear and handbrake are handled for you. Automatic: select Drive (1) or Reverse (R) and release the handbrake (H) yourself, as in a real car. Either way the car then creeps slowly on its own. Tap or hold Space to brake. You rarely need the gas while parking.

      Manual: clutch control (the key to slow manoeuvres)

      1. Clutch fully down (Q), select the gear.
      2. Press B to bring the clutch near the biting point, then scroll up one notch at a time until the revs dip and the clutch bar turns orange (biting).
      3. Release the handbrake (H). The car creeps: scroll up a notch to go a little faster, down a notch to slow. Brake (Space) with the clutch down to stop.
      4. Lift the clutch too fast and you'll stall, just like the real car. Brake without the clutch down and you'll stall too.

      Observations are scored

      Like the examiner, the game checks you look all round before moving and keep looking behind while reversing. Use the mouse in the driver's seat: left blind spot, over your left shoulder through the rear window, right blind spot, and the mirrors. The chips at the top light up when you've checked them.
